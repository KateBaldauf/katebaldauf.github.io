Why organize it this way?

Answer the practical questions first.

A visitor needs to know when it is, where to go, and whether food is available. Keeping those details together helps them decide whether to come.

The actual work

Most of my Grainworks event pages were one long page. When an event needed additional context, supporting pages—such as band pages—provided more detail.

I also worked through practical improvements to the existing WordPress site: simplifying the hours display, removing an unnecessary heading, improving mobile spacing, and correcting uneven button heights.

What this demonstrates

  • Organizing content around a visitor’s questions.
  • Choosing when to keep information together and when to link out.
  • Maintaining an inherited website and refining small usability details.

The interactive example demonstrates my approach. It is not an archived screenshot or a claim of measured conversion improvement.
